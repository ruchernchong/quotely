> Listen, nobody hands you a key to the 'Be Your Own Boss' kingdom. You've got to pick the lock yourself, maybe even kick down a few wobbly doors. Stop waiting for permission to unfurl your quirky flag and declare your personal republic. Life's too short to dance to someone else's kazoo. Go ahead, make your own weird music, trip over your own two feet with gusto, and remember: the only chains worth breaking are the ones you imagine. Your wild, independent spirit isn't a houseplant; it's a jungle. Let it grow.
