---
title: "Herding Cats, Leading Humans"
date: "2026-10-08"
---

> A leader often realizes their true power isn't in commanding, but in subtly suggesting the direction while making everyone feel they picked it themselves. It's like being the conductor of an orchestra where every musician believes they're improvising. The trick is to influence the collective song without anyone noticing you're holding the sheet music. That's real leadership, delightfully disguised.
