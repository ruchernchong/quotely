> True freedom isn't found in breaking external chains, but in dismantling the mental constructs that keep us tethered. It's an inward rebellion, a defiant refusal to inherit another's script. My spirit craves its own wild compass, unburdened by the echoes of expectation, charting a course known only to the heart's sovereign whisper. To be truly free is to be undeniably, unreservedly oneself, a universe unto itself.
