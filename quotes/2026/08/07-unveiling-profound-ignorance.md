> The profoundest wisdom often hides in the shadows of what we claim to know. Embrace the elegant ignorance that unveils deeper truths. For only by shedding the comfort of certainty can the unknown whispers of understanding truly resonate, shaping knowledge not as a destination, but an eternal, intriguing labyrinth.
