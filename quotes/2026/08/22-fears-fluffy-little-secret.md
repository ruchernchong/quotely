---
title: "Fear's Fluffy Little Secret"
date: "2026-08-22"
---

> They say 'face your fears' as if fear were a monstrous beast. Nonsense! Fear is often just an overzealous internal alarm, mistaking a butterfly for a dragon. It's not trying to stop you; it's just really bad at distinguishing danger from a Tuesday. The trick isn't to slay it, but to gently pat its head and say, 'Thanks for the warning, pal, I'm just getting a coffee.' Courage isn't the absence of this bumbling alarm; it's the ability to appreciate its dramatic flair without letting it dictate your morning. So next time fear screams, try offering it a biscuit instead of a sword.
